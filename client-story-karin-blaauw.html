How Karin Blaauw helps Hibiscus Coast sellers know where they stand

Selling a home is full of moments where the owner doesn’t know what’s happening: what the house is really worth, who came through on Sunday, and what an offer actually says. Karin builds her whole process around closing those gaps.

In short

Karin Blaauw is a licensed salesperson with Harcourts Cooper & Co in Silverdale, working across the Hibiscus Coast. Her sellers get a written valuation built from settled sales, a campaign plan they agree before launch, open homes she runs herself, and a written update every week until settlement.

Valuations you can check

Karin prices a home from recent comparable settled sales, “not asking prices or online estimates”, and gives the appraisal in writing with those comparables shown. Sellers can see the evidence behind the number instead of taking it on trust.

A plan agreed before anything goes live

Each campaign is planned with the seller first. In her words: “We agree the plan together before anything goes live.” The plan covers professional photography (with drone shots where they help), the listing portals, the Harcourts network, and targeted social and print.

She runs the open homes herself

“I run open homes personally — never handed off.” Every buyer who comes through gets a follow-up call within 24 hours, and the seller gets a written summary after each open home.

Weekly updates, in writing

Sellers hear from Karin every week in writing, by email or WhatsApp, with a call whenever something material changes. Offers come in writing too, “in plain English”. When several offers arrive at once, the Harcourts multi-offer process is followed exactly.

Still there after the offer

Karin stays involved through condition dates, builder’s reports, finance approval and the pre-settlement inspection, so the seller isn’t left alone between “sold” and settlement day.

Local, not generic

Karin works across the Hibiscus Coast and the Whangaparāoa Peninsula, and she writes about each suburb for the people selling there. Her Stanmore Bay guide is a good example. She spent two decades in financial services before real estate, and she speaks English and Afrikaans, which matters to many families moving to the coast from South Africa.

What Eko does for Karin

Eko Media built and runs karinblaauw.co.nz. Each suburb has its own guide, each sale gets its own sold-story page, and her Google Business Profile, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn posts link back to the matching page. Our job is to make sure that when a Hibiscus Coast owner asks Google or an AI assistant about selling, the clear, specific information Karin already gives her sellers is easy to find.

Karin Blaauw is a Licensed Salesperson under the Real Estate Agents Act 2008 (licence 20087155, on the public register at rea.govt.nz).
