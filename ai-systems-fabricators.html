Fabricator FAQs

Do I have to buy the whole fabricator package?

No. AI visibility, enquiry handling and the dashboard can each be done on their own. Most fabricators start with the free Systems Leak Check or an AI-Citation Audit and add the other parts once they have seen where the work is being lost.

Will it work with our quoting and job software?

We confirm that during the review, before you commit to a build. Where your software has an official API or a native integration we connect to it. Where it has no reliable way to connect, we say so rather than build something fragile.

We are part of a manufacturer or franchise network. Can you still help?

Yes. Your business keeps its own name, its own Google Business Profile and its own results. We report for your business only and never pool your numbers with other fabricators.

How does AI decide which fabricator to name?

AI assistants draw on what they can read and trust about a business: its website, its Google Business Profile, its reviews, and the directories and articles that mention it. If those sources are thin, inconsistent or unreadable, the assistant names someone else. More in Does AI use my Google Business Profile?

Can you guarantee more leads?

No. No one controls how an AI assistant phrases its answer. We make your business readable, accurate and present where AI looks, and make sure the enquiries you do get are not lost. We report what changes, dated and engine by engine.

What does it cost?

Every part is scoped and quoted before we start. The Systems Leak Check and the AI Readiness Check are free.
