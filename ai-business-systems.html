AI business systems FAQs

Do I need to be a GEO client to use these services?

No. Dashboards, integrations and process reviews are standalone services. Some clients use them alongside GEO, because getting named by AI brings in enquiries and good systems make sure those enquiries are handled well, but neither depends on the other.

Where does AI fit in?

Only where it saves time on a task that can be checked: sorting and routing enquiries, summarising notes or calls, drafting replies, and writing a plain-English summary of what moved on a dashboard. Anything customer-facing or financial has a person approve it before it goes out.

What software do you work with?

Most cloud software that offers an API or a native integration: CRMs, accounting, job management, booking, forms, email and spreadsheets. We build automated workflows in n8n and connect through official APIs where they exist. We confirm what is possible for your specific tools during the review, before you commit to a build.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Where we can, accounts are set up in your name, and every build comes with documentation and a walkthrough, so the system is not locked to us.

How much time will this save us?

We won't put a number on it before we have looked. The process review records what we measured and what we estimate, and labels which is which, so you can judge the payoff of each fix before committing to it.

What does it cost?

Every project is scoped and quoted before we start, so you know the cost before any work begins. Builds are quoted from what the review finds.

Do you work with businesses outside Auckland?

Yes. Eko Media is based in Auckland and works with businesses across New Zealand and Australia. Most of the work is done remotely, with calls or on-site time where mapping a process needs it.

What's slowing your business down?

Tell us in a couple of lines which tools you use and where the manual work is. We'll reply with whether a review makes sense.
