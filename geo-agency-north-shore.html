GEO and AI search marketing on Auckland’s North Shore

More people now ask ChatGPT or Google’s AI Mode who to use before they scroll a list of links. The answer names a handful of businesses. This page is about how a business on the North Shore becomes one of them.

By Eko Media · Reviewed by Freddie Berning, founder · Published 19 November 2026

The short answer

Eko Media is an Auckland GEO agency that works with businesses across the North Shore, from Takapuna and Devonport to Albany and Browns Bay. GEO (generative engine optimisation) makes your business readable and checkable for ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews and Perplexity, so they can name you when someone asks who to use on the Shore.

Why does a North Shore business need to show up in AI answers?

People ask with a place in the question: “good accountant in Takapuna”, “physio near Albany”, “builder on the North Shore”. A search engine shows ten results. An AI assistant gives one answer that names two or three businesses and explains why. If you aren’t one of the names, the customer often never sees you at all.

The Shore is crowded with capable businesses, so being good isn’t enough on its own. AI picks the ones it can read and confirm: your services and suburbs written as plain text, a Google Business Profile that matches your site, and other sources that say the same thing about you.

What did we find when we searched for GEO on the North Shore?

On 19 Nov 2026 we ran two live Google searches set to the North Shore. “GEO agency North Shore Auckland” on desktop mixed GEO with geography: a spatial-services press release and a list of North Shore surveyors came up next to national SEO lists and an Australian agency’s Instagram post. “AI search marketing North Shore” on mobile returned social posts and agencies in Canada and elsewhere, with no North Shore business at all.

So nobody has answered this question for the Shore yet, and “GEO” on its own is ambiguous, which is why a local page has to say generative engine optimisation and AI search in full. The questions Google suggested were about the best SEO company and what SEO costs in New Zealand; we answer both below. We didn’t capture an AI Overview for either search, so we make no claim about what Google’s AI says.

What does GEO work look like for a North Shore business?

  • Name the suburbs you really serve. One clear page per service, with places such as Takapuna, Milford or Albany written out as text, not just “Auckland”.
  • Make your Google Business Profile match. Same name, address, phone, hours and service areas as your site. Google’s AI features draw on it directly.
  • Get confirmed by local sources. Directory listings, your industry body, and your local business association or town centre group where you’re a member.
  • Ask for reviews that name the job and the place. “Fixed our deck in Devonport” tells AI far more than five stars on their own.
  • Let AI crawlers in, and mark up the facts. Check that robots.txt and your host don’t block OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot (which bots matter), and add schema for your business, services and area.
