How JJ van der Westhuizen guides Hibiscus Coast home buyers from first chat to settlement

Getting a home loan can feel like a black box: which lender, which structure, what paperwork, and what happens after the keys. JJ sets out the whole path up front, so his clients know what comes next at every step.

In short

Mortgages with JJ is the practice of JJ van der Westhuizen, a Mortgage Adviser based in Orewa. Every client follows the same four steps: a free first conversation, a strategy and lender match, the application and approval, then settlement and a yearly review.

Four clear steps

JJ's process is the same for every client, and it is written on his site for anyone to read:

  • Free consultation. A 30-minute chat to understand the situation and the goal.
  • Strategy and lender match. In his words: “I compare your situation across the lender panel, structure the deal, and recommend the best path.”
  • Application and approval. JJ prepares and submits the application and manages the conversation with the lender.
  • Settlement and ongoing review. After settlement he checks in every year “to keep your loan working”.

Someone who picks up the phone

“From the first conversation to the day the funds settle, you have someone in your corner who actually picks up the phone.” JJ meets clients in person on the Hibiscus Coast, or works fully remotely for clients elsewhere in New Zealand.

Built around the client

“My approach is always client-centric: every solution is custom-built to meet your goals while staying sustainable long term.” Before advising, JJ spent years in commercial banking, and he works with first home buyers, growing families, investors, self-employed clients and South African families settling in New Zealand.

First home buyers know what to bring

His first home buyers page lists the documents to gather before the first meeting, from ID and payslips to bank and KiwiSaver statements, so there are no surprises later.

What Eko does for JJ

Eko Media built and runs mortgageswithjj.co.nz. Each area and service has its own page, his monthly guides answer the questions local buyers actually ask, and the compliance wording is kept identical across the site. Our job is to make sure that when someone on the Hibiscus Coast asks Google or an AI assistant about getting a mortgage, the clear, step-by-step information JJ already gives his clients is easy to find.
