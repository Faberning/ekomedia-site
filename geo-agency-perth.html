What does GEO work look like for a Perth business?

  • Name the suburbs you really serve. One clear page per service, with places such as Joondalup, Fremantle or Midland written out as text, not just “Perth” or “WA”.
  • Make your Google Business Profile match. Same name, address or service area, phone, hours and categories as your site. Google’s AI features draw on it directly.
  • Get confirmed by other sources. Directory listings, your industry body and local business groups, all saying the same thing about you.
  • Ask for reviews that name the job and the place. “Rewired our place in Bassendean” tells AI far more than five stars on their own.
  • Let AI crawlers in, and mark up the facts. Check that robots.txt and your host don’t block OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot (which bots matter), and add schema for your business, services and area.
