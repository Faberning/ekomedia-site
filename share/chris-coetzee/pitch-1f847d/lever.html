Your questions · income protection

What does "own occupation" mean in income protection?

Chris Coetzee, Financial Adviser · DormFIN Financial Services, Silverdale · general information
The short answerIt's one way an income protection policy decides whether you can't work. With own-occupation wording, you're assessed on whether you can do the main duties of your own job, not whether you could do some other job.

What does "own occupation" mean?

Income protection pays a monthly benefit if illness or injury stops you working. Every policy has to define "can't work". Own-occupation wording looks at the job you actually do. If you can no longer do its main duties, you may be able to claim, even if you could do a different kind of work.

How is it different from "any occupation"?

Broader wordings ask whether you could do any job you're reasonably suited to by education, training or experience. That's a harder test to meet, because another job you could do may count against the claim.

Who usually asks about it?

People whose income depends on specialised skills: dentists, surgeons, engineers, architects, and self-employed people whose work is hard to swap for something else. For them, the difference between the two wordings can decide a claim.

Does the definition stay the same for the whole claim?

Not always. Some policies use own-occupation wording for a set time, then switch to a broader test. The policy wording says when, so read that section, not just the name of the cover.

Does it change the cost?

It can. Premiums depend on the insurer, your occupation and the definition you choose. An adviser can show you how the options compare for your situation.

What should I check before choosing a policy?

  • How "can't work" is defined, and whether that changes after a set time
  • The waiting period before payments start
  • The benefit period: how long payments can last
  • How the benefit is worked out, as an agreed amount or based on your earnings at claim time
  • What happens if you change roles or reduce your hours
