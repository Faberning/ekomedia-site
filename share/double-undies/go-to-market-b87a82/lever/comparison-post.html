What's the difference between Double Undies and Modibodi?

They're built for different jobs. Modibodi started with period underwear, and its PUMA x Modibodi range takes that into sport and swimming in three absorbencies. Double Undies was designed for women who move, with one pair made to handle light bladder leaks, periods and sweat during high-impact training.

Both are washable and reusable, both make thongs, and both are designed to sit smoothly under activewear. The differences are in the gusset, the fabric next to your skin and how much each style holds.

How much does each one hold?

At the middle level they're close. Double Undies holds over 30 ml, about six tampons' worth. Modibodi's Moderate holds 30 ml, or up to six tampons based on a 5 ml tampon.

03

Which one shows less under gym tights?

Both are designed not to. Double Undies have smooth, laser-cut edges and bonded seams. The PUMA x Modibodi styles use bonded seams or 360° knitting, sold as "no VPL".

Fit matters more than brand here. Underwear that's too tight shifts when you move, and that's when lines and gaps appear.

04

What about the waistband?

Double Undies have a wider, supportive band. It can feel firm at first and relaxes with wear, and if you're between sizes the brand says to size up.

Some PUMA x Modibodi styles have a wide waistband that Modibodi says sits flat so it won't dig in. If you train while bloated, try a size and style that feels comfortable sitting down as well as standing.

05

Cotton or merino: which handles sweat better?

They take different routes. Double Undies put 100% cotton against your skin, with a moisture-wicking outer fabric and antibacterial, odour-control layers. Modibodi uses a 100% merino wool top layer, which it says controls odour and wicks moisture.

Neither of these two ranges uses bamboo in the layer against your skin. Cotton feels familiar and soft; merino is known for handling odour. It comes down to what you like wearing.

Are they bulky for running or cycling?

Neither is designed to be. Modibodi describes its Moderate lining as a slim 2 mm. Double Undies says its pairs are slightly thicker than regular underwear because of the absorbent layers, but smooth and light once they're on.

If chafing on long runs or rides is your worry, look for a smooth, seam-free finish and a gusset that stays where it should.

07

Which is better for bladder leaks during exercise?

Double Undies is designed around that moment: the jump, the sprint, the heavy lift. Its winged gusset adds surface area and absorbency where you need it most, and the brand is clear it's for exercise-related leaks, not a full bladder release.

Modibodi also covers bladder leaks and makes incontinence underwear, but its PUMA active range is sold mainly as period protection.

How do you wash them?

Almost the same way. Both brands say to wash cold on a gentle cycle, skip fabric softener and never tumble dry. Modibodi suggests a cold rinse first, and Double Undies says to wash before the first wear.

Each pair of Double Undies comes with a waterproof bag, made from recycled plastic bottles, for the trip home from the gym.

09

So which should you choose?

Choose by the job you need it to do. Plenty of women own both.

Double Undies, if

  • You train high-impact: skipping, running, lifting
  • You want light bladder-leak cover in the same pair as period and sweat cover
  • You want a thong that holds more than a light level
  • You prefer cotton against your skin

Modibodi, if

  • You need heavier period or overnight absorbency
  • You want merino against your skin
  • You want period swimwear or matching crops
  • You want more cuts to choose from
