Know your container

Floor-loaded or palletised

Floor-loaded

Packed with loose cartons from floor to roof. Each carton is unloaded by hand and stacked onto pallets. It fits more product in the container but takes longer to unload.

Palletised

Arrives with the goods already on pallets and is unloaded by forklift, which is much quicker.

Your questions answered

What Auckland importers ask before they book

What is container devanning?‡

+

Devanning is unloading the goods from a shipping container at a warehouse. At Highbrook the stock is taken out, checked against your paperwork and then cross docked, picked or sent straight on, depending on what you need.

How long does it take to devan a 20ft or 40ft container?‡

+

It depends on the container size, how many cartons are inside, how many different product lines there are and whether the goods arrive on pallets or loose. Palletised containers come off with a forklift and are much quicker than loose, floor-loaded cartons stacked by hand. Send us your packing list and we will give you a time for your container.

Floor-loaded or palletised: what is the difference?

+

A floor-loaded container is packed with loose cartons from floor to roof, so each carton is unloaded by hand and stacked onto pallets. A palletised container arrives with the goods already on pallets and is unloaded by forklift. Floor-loading fits more product in the container but takes longer to unload.

How much does container devanning cost in Auckland?‡

+

The price depends on the container size, whether it is floor-loaded or palletised, the carton count and what happens to the stock next. Tell us about your container and we will send a quote.

What happens to my stock after the container is unloaded?

+

At Highbrook, stock can go into cross dock and straight back out, into order picking (including pick and pack) for your customers, or into nationwide distribution through the MOVe freight network of 17 freight branches. We can also load containers for export.

Can MOVe collect the container from the port?‡

+

MOVe Freight runs full container load services, so the container can come to Highbrook and the empty can go back, arranged by one company. Ask us about the port and container park charges that apply to your shipment.
