What does a mortgage broker do?

A mortgage adviser compares home loans from a range of lenders, helps you prepare your application and manages it through to settlement.

  • Works out what you can borrow and how to structure it.
  • Puts your application to the lenders that suit your situation.
  • Stays with you after settlement, including when your rate comes up for refixing.

Is it cheaper to use a mortgage broker in New Zealand?

Most mortgage advisers in New Zealand are paid by the lender, so many clients pay no adviser fee. Any fee or clawback must be disclosed to you before advice is given.
