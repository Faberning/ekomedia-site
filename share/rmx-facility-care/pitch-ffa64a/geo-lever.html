What does commercial office cleaning include?

A regular office clean covers desks, kitchens, bathrooms, floors and rubbish, with deeper jobs like windows and carpets added to your regular clean as needed.

  • Every visit: kitchens, bathrooms, bins, vacuuming and mopping, wiping touch points.
  • Weekly or monthly: glass, skirting, high dusting, appliance cleaning.
  • Added to your regular clean: window cleaning, carpet cleaning and post-construction cleans.

How often should an office be cleaned?

Most busy offices need kitchens and bathrooms cleaned daily, with a full clean once or twice a week.

A small office with a few staff can often work on two or three visits a week. A shared floor or a customer-facing site usually needs a daily visit.

Not sure how often your site needs a visit? We'll suggest a schedule on a free walk-through.

What affects the cost of commercial cleaning in Auckland?

Cost depends on floor area, how often we visit, what's in scope and the hours we work. We quote after a free walk-through.

The walk-through lets us see the site, agree a checklist and give you a fixed quote for that scope.

Can cleaning be done after hours?

Yes. Most office cleaning happens early morning or in the evening, so staff and customers aren't disturbed.
