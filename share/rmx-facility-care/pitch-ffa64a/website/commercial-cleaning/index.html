What does commercial cleaning include?

A regular commercial clean covers kitchens, washrooms, floors, bins and touchpoints, with windows, carpets and deep cleans added to the schedule as needed.

  • Every visit: kitchen and lunchroom, washroom sanitation, rubbish removal, vacuuming and mopping, touchpoint sanitisation.
  • Scheduled: window cleaning, carpets, high dusting and deep cleans.
  • Projects: post-construction and builders cleans, final sparkle cleans.

Which sites do you clean?

Offices and corporate buildings, retail and showrooms, medical and healthcare, education, hospitality, industrial and warehouse sites, and shared or body corporate facilities.

How often should a commercial site be cleaned?

Busy sites usually need washrooms and kitchens done daily, with a full clean once or twice a week. Quieter sites can work on two or three visits a week.

Can cleaning happen after hours?

Yes. Most commercial cleaning is done early morning or in the evening, so staff and customers are not disturbed.

What affects the cost of commercial cleaning in Auckland?

Floor area, how often we visit, what is in scope and the hours we work. We quote a fixed price after a free walk-through of your site.

Can you supply and restock our cleaning products?

Yes. We restock consumables like paper towels, soap and bin liners during your scheduled clean, and you can order chemicals and supplies from our online shop.
