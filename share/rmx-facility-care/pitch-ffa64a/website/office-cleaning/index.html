What does office cleaning include?

Desks and touchpoints, kitchens and lunchrooms, washrooms, floors and bins on every visit, plus windows and carpets on a schedule.

How often should an office be cleaned?

Most offices with a busy kitchen and washrooms need a daily visit. Smaller teams often work on two or three visits a week.

Do your cleaners work after hours?

Yes. We clean early morning or in the evening so your team walks into a clean office.

Can you keep our washrooms stocked?

Yes. Paper towels, toilet tissue, hand soap and bin liners are restocked during the clean, on one invoice with the cleaning.
