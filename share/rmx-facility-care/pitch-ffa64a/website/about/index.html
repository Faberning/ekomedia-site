About Us

RMX Facility Care is an Auckland-based facility services company delivering reliable, professional, and high-quality commercial cleaning, post-construction cleaning, and facility maintenance solutions across the Auckland region.

At RMX, we understand that clean, safe, and well-maintained environments create better experiences for staff, customers, tenants, and visitors. Our approach combines professionalism, attention to detail, responsive communication, and dependable service delivery.

We pride ourselves on building long-term relationships with our clients by consistently providing tailored facility solutions that meet the unique needs of every site.
