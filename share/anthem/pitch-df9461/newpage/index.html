Questions

Choosing a PR agency in Auckland

The questions comms and marketing leads ask before they brief an agency, answered straight.

Ask us yours
How do I choose a PR agency in Auckland?

Shortlist three agencies that have done work like yours. Give each the same short brief, then meet the people who would actually run your account, not just the pitch team. Pick the team whose thinking fits your problem.

What should I ask a PR agency before I sign?

Who works on our account day to day? Which organisations like ours have you worked with? How will we know it is working, and what will you report each month? What happens if a crisis lands on a Friday night?

Boutique or big network agency: which is better?

Neither is better in general. Boutique agencies often put senior people on your account. Networks can bring reach across countries. Choose the team that fits the work you need done.

How do PR agencies charge?

Usually a monthly retainer for ongoing work, or a project fee for a launch or campaign. The price follows the scope and the seniority of the people involved, so ask for both in writing.

How long before PR shows results?

Some wins come in weeks, like a story placed or a crisis contained. Reputation builds over months. Agree what you will measure at three, six and twelve months before you start.

What does a PR agency actually do?

It helps an organisation earn attention and trust: media coverage, crisis and issues work, government relations, corporate announcements, and the messages behind them.

When should we call a PR agency in a crisis?

Before you say anything in public. An hour of advice early is worth more than a week of repair later, so keep an agency's after-hours number on file.
