How do AI shopping agents choose which business to use?

The next step after AI answers is AI that acts. Instead of listing a few names, an agent shortlists businesses for the buyer, asks for quotes and, where it can, books. This is what that means for a New Zealand service business.

The short answer

An AI shopping agent doesn’t just name businesses, it shortlists them and can take the next step for the buyer: asking for a quote, checking availability or completing a booking. It filters on the buyer’s constraints first (place, timing, what’s included, terms) and uses price to split options that look the same. So the business that gets picked is the one whose facts an agent can read, check and act on without a phone call.

What is an AI shopping agent, and how is it different from an AI answer?

When someone asks ChatGPT or Google’s AI Mode “who does house washing in Orewa?”, they get an answer: a few names and some reasons. When they ask an assistant to “get me three quotes for a house wash in Orewa next week”, it behaves as an agent. It compares, filters and takes the next step on the buyer’s behalf.

Can an AI agent complete the purchase or booking itself?

It’s started to. In September 2025 OpenAI and Stripe launched Instant Checkout in ChatGPT, built on an open standard called the Agentic Commerce Protocol. In November 2025 Google added agentic checkout and an AI that calls local stores to check price and stock. Both launched for US users first.

For most NZ service businesses (trades, advisers, agents, local services) the step that matters today is simpler: an agent asking you for a quote or a booking. If that step needs a phone call during business hours, a login, or a form that doesn’t work, the agent has nothing to report back, and the buyer sees the business that did answer.

How do I get an AI agent to pick my business rather than a competitor?

Agents work through the buyer’s constraints before they look at price. If you look the same as everyone else on the facts they can read, price is the only thing left to compare. So make the things you win on readable:

  • State your constraints as plain facts. Services, the suburbs and towns you cover, hours, typical lead time and what’s included, written as text on your site and marked up with schema, not hidden in an image or a PDF.
  • Give it proof it can check somewhere else. Reviews that name the job and the place, a consistent Google Business Profile, directory listings and licences. An agent trusts what other sources confirm.
  • Make the next step machine-completable. A plain quote or booking form with an instant confirmation, and no CAPTCHA wall before first contact.
  • Reply fast, and say how fast. An agent that contacts several businesses can only recommend the ones that answered. Publish a reply time you can keep.
  • Write your terms down. Cancellation, rework and warranty in plain words. Agents read them for the buyer.

How does a local business compete when the agent also checks overseas sites?

For anything that has to happen in a place, such as a service, an install or a booking, the buyer’s location is the first filter. An offshore site can’t send someone to Silverdale on Thursday. Local businesses win this filter only if the agent can see it: name your service area by suburb and town, show where you’ve worked, and keep your address, phone and hours identical everywhere they appear. For products, the local edge is delivery time, returns and after-sales support. Again, it only counts if it’s written down.

Where does a human stay in the loop?

Buyers usually still approve the final choice and the payment, and plenty of people want to talk to a person before they commit. That works in your favour. The agent gets you onto the shortlist, and your reply and your people win the job. On your side, keep a person approving anything an AI drafts for customers, such as quotes, prices and promises. Watch: AI shopping agents in one minute

The honest bit. Agent shopping is early, especially in New Zealand, and the tools change month to month. There’s no reliable NZ figure yet for how many purchases agents make, and nobody can promise an agent will pick you. What you control is whether your facts are readable, your proof is checkable and your next step can be completed. Those are the same foundations that get you named in AI answers today.
